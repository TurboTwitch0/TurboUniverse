# TurboUniverse imagery

The images in this directory were created as original artwork with OpenAI image generation for TurboUniverse. They depict imagined natural landscapes and astronomy-inspired scenes; the space images are artistic illustrations rather than NASA photographs or scientific observations.

The landscape prompts explicitly excluded people, silhouettes, buildings, and vehicles. The space prompts excluded people, spacecraft, writing, and logos. The resulting images were visually inspected and packaged as compressed WebP assets for the website. Original generated PNGs remain in `/workspace/generated_images`.

| Asset | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| `earth-hero.webp` | Terracotta sandstone canyon and mountain plateau at sunset |
| `earth-detail.webp` | Foggy mountain ridges, dusty sage pine forest, and ochre rocks |
| `space-01.webp` | Ringed planet in a warm star field |
| `space-02.webp` | Rust-colored cratered moon and a distant crescent planet |
| `space-03.webp` | Copper and indigo star-forming nebula |
| `space-04.webp` | Cream-gold spiral galaxy |
| `space-05.webp` | Dark planetary eclipse with a luminous crescent rim |
| `space-06.webp` | Dense Milky Way river and distant galaxies |

## Seasonal theme expansion

Two additional original landscapes were generated with OpenAI image generation for the seasonal theme system. Both explicitly exclude people, silhouettes, buildings, vehicles, and signage, and were visually inspected before WebP packaging. The directory now contains ten original generated images.

| Asset | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| `earth-spring.webp` | Sage-green alpine meadow, small wildflowers, golden dawn, and misty mountain ridges |
| `earth-winter.webp` | Snowy pine valley, icy blue shadows, mist, and warm sunrise along mountain peaks |
